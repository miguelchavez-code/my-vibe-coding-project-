# Show & Swap Notes

> Module 2 · Validation. Put the prototype in front of a peer. Their reaction is the data.

## What I showed

https://the-internal-tool-nobody-uses.replit.app — A prototype testing whether focused daily follow-ups and one-tap logging help sales reps use the CRM instead of a separate spreadsheet.

## What my partner understood (in their words)

You’re testing whether making the CRM useful for my daily work, not just management reporting, would make me come back and stop maintaining a spreadsheet.
The proposed kill switch is to rethink the approach if, after four weeks of actual use, repeat usage and logging don’t improve and spreadsheet reliance stays flat.

## Where they got confused / pushed back

The deal amounts and next actions help me decide what to work on. But I wasn’t sure what ‘Log activity’ actually recorded. Did I call someone, send something, or just mark a task complete?. I could see myself trying this, but finishing one task here doesn’t prove I’d give up my spreadsheet.

## What I'll change as a result

Make quick logging outcome-neutral: record that an activity was logged without assuming the rep connected with the customer. Keep specific outcomes available in details. This preserves the one-tap experience while making the activity history more trustworthy.
