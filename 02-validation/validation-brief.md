# Validation Brief

> Module 2 · Validation. Frame the riskiest assumption, then build to test it. **This locks your scenario, no switching after M2.**

## Scenario

Scenario 02 · The Internal Tool Nobody Uses

## Riskiest assumption

_The one belief that, if false, sinks the idea._

I believe sales reps avoid consistently updating the CRM because it adds work outside their normal selling workflow. If we make it easier to capture and retrieve customer information during that workflow, reps will use it more consistently and keep records more complete.

## Hypothesis

> We believe **Replace the cluttered CRM home with a focused view of today’s follow-ups and one-tap activity logging.** will cause **Reps manage their daily follow-ups in the CRM rather than maintaining a separate spreadsheet.** for **Sales reps who currently track follow-ups outside the CRM.**. We'll know we're right when **More reps use the CRM on multiple days each week, a higher share of follow-ups are logged, and fewer reps rely on shadow spreadsheets.**.

## Risk type

- [x] Value (do they want it?)
- [x] Usability (can they use it?)
- [ ] Feasibility (can we build it?)
- [ ] Viability (should we?)

## Kill switch

_The result that would make you stop or pivot._

After a four-week pilot, if reps aren’t using the simplified home more consistently and still rely on shadow spreadsheets for follow-ups, we won’t roll it out further. We’ll revisit whether follow-ups and logging are the real barriers to adoption.

## The three ingredients

- **Real data (domain metrics):**
  - 11 min, Avg. task time for "update a deal" vs. 2-min target.
  - 18%, Weekly active adoption across licensed seats.
  - 8, Required fields to log a single deal update.
  - 63%, Reps keeping a shadow spreadsheet alongside the CRM.
  - 2.1 / 5, Internal CSAT for the tool.
- **User voice (verbatim quotes):**
  - "I never know where the thing I need lives. Every screen looks like a settings page.", Sales rep, onboarded but inactive
  - "It's faster to keep my deals in a spreadsheet than to fight the CRM's eight required fields.", Account exec, 2 logins / month
  - "Logging activity feels like data entry for management, not something that helps me sell.", Senior AE
- **Hypothesis (above):** ✓
